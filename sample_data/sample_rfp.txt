REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — RFP-2026-014
Acme Insurance Group: Claims Automation Platform Modernization

SECTION 3 — SCOPE AND TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

3.1 The Vendor shall design and implement a cloud-native claims intake platform
    supporting a minimum of 50,000 claims per day.
3.2 The solution must integrate with the existing Guidewire ClaimCenter system
    via published APIs.
3.3 The platform shall provide AI-assisted document classification for claims
    correspondence with a minimum accuracy of 95%.
3.4 The Vendor should provide a mobile-first claimant self-service portal.
3.5 All data at rest and in transit must be encrypted (AES-256 / TLS 1.2+).
3.6 The solution must be hosted within the continental United States and comply
    with SOC 2 Type II requirements.
3.7 The Vendor shall provide fraud-detection scoring on all incoming claims in
    real time (sub-second latency preferred).
3.8 The system should support Spanish-language claimant communications.
3.9 The Vendor must provide 24x7 production support with a 15-minute response
    SLA for Severity 1 incidents.
3.10 The Vendor shall migrate 7 years of historical claims data (approximately
     40 TB) with full audit traceability.

SECTION 4 — DELIVERY AND COMMERCIAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 The Vendor must complete implementation within 12 months of contract award.
4.2 The Vendor shall provide unlimited liability coverage for data breaches
    arising from Vendor negligence.
4.3 All intellectual property developed under this engagement shall transfer to
    Acme Insurance Group.
4.4 The Vendor should describe its approach to knowledge transfer and training.
4.5 Proposals must include three references from insurance-industry clients of
    similar size.
4.6 The Vendor must maintain adequate staffing as deemed appropriate by Acme.
